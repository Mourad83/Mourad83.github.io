HR 360
Workforce & People Analytics

A Power BI solution dedicated to workforce management. HR 360 consolidates workforce, absenteeism, turnover, performance, compensation, training and employee-journey data into decision-ready insights.

Main toolPower BI

Project context

Business need

Provide HR leadership and managers with a consolidated view of workforce, employee movements, absenteeism, retention, performance and employee development.

BI approach

Build a multi-fact HR Power BI model with shared dimensions, Power Query transformations, DAX measures, multi-page navigation and a detailed individual employee profile.

Technical highlights

Data model & calculations

Multi-fact HR model using Employee, Date, Department and Position dimensions, with controlled grains and DAX measures for workforce, turnover, retention, absenteeism, payroll and performance.

User experience & analysis

Employee 360 combines identity, job, manager, contract, work mode, career level, performance, compensation, skills, education, training, evaluations and exit information.
